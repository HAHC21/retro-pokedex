# How to Upload Your PC Box Screenshots to Retro Pokédex

You can automatically update your Living Dex without typing in names manually! All you need is a screenshot of your PC box or Pokémon HOME grid.

Follow these simple steps to transfer your screenshots from your console to your phone or computer.

---

## Step 1: Capture Your Box Screenshot

1. Open your game (*Pokémon Scarlet*, *Pokémon Violet*, or *Pokémon HOME*) and navigate to the **PC Box** or **Grid View** you want to scan.
2. Make sure the 30-slot grid is fully visible on your screen.
3. Press the **Capture Button** (the square button on the **Left Joy-Con** or left side of your controller) to take a screenshot.

---

## Step 2: Transfer the Image to Your Phone

You can send the screenshot to your phone using either of the two official methods below.

### Option A: The Nintendo Switch Smart App (Easiest)

1. Go to the console **HOME Menu** and select the **Album** icon.
2. Select your PC box screenshot and press **A**.
3. Select **Sharing and Editing** $\rightarrow$ **Upload to Smart Device**.
4. Choose **Upload**.
5. Open the **Nintendo Switch App** on your phone.
6. Tap the **Album** tab at the bottom right.
7. Tap **Select**, choose your screenshot, and hit **Save** to download it to your phone's photo library.

---

### Option B: Direct QR Code Scan (No App Required)

1. Go to the console **HOME Menu** and select the **Album** icon.
2. Select your PC box screenshot and press **A**.
3. Select **Sharing and Editing** $\rightarrow$ **Send to Smartphone**.
   * *Tip: If you took multiple box screenshots, choose "Send a Batch" to send up to 10 photos at once!*
4. Open your phone's camera app and scan the **first QR code** on your TV or screen. Tap the prompt to join the temporary console Wi-Fi network.
5. Scan the **second QR code** that appears. This will open a browser page on your phone displaying your image(s).
6. Long-press the screenshot and select **Save to Photos** or **Download Image**.

---

## Step 3: Scan on Retro Pokédex

1. Open the **Retro Pokédex Box Scanner** on your phone or PC.
2. Tap **Upload Screenshot** and select your saved PC box image.
3. Review the quick 30-slot preview modal to confirm your Pokémon.
4. Tap **Confirm & Sync to Living Dex**!

---

*Need help? Make sure your phone's Wi-Fi is enabled when using the QR code method, or verify that your console is connected to the internet when using the Nintendo Switch App upload.*
