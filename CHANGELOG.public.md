# What’s new

Updates you can see in Retro Pokédex. Releases that only changed hosting, search listings, or internal data are left out.

## 0.7.4 — September 24, 2026

- Fossil Pokémon have a bronze border, and starters have a gray one. The name and Pokédex number use that same color.
- Pseudo-legendary violet covers the whole evolutionary line.
- A species page shows a rarity label between the Pokédex number and the types. An info button explains that rarity.

## 0.7.3 — September 24, 2026

- Legendary Pokémon have a gold border on Pokédex cards, checklist rows, and the species page.
- Mythical Pokémon have a dark red border in those same places.
- Pseudo-legendaries have a violet border.

## 0.7.2 — September 24, 2026

- The dashboard lists Mega Evolutions, Gigantamax, and Regional Forms. Each box opens that list in the Pokédex.
- Mega Evolutions and Gigantamax show their colored sprite and no catch marks, since they cannot be caught on their own.
- A Mega or Gigantamax page leaves out the Pokédex entry, abilities and natures, wild locations, moves, and your specimens.

## 0.7.1 — September 24, 2026

- A changelog page lists what changed in each release. Open it from the footer.
- Male and Female buttons appear only on a Pokémon’s page.

## 0.7.0 — September 24, 2026

- Alternate forms now have their own details: Mega Evolutions, regional forms, Gigantamax, and similar.
- Switch forms in the Pokédex, the checklist, a Pokémon’s page, and your collection.
- Sprites show the Pokémon’s gender when the male and female look different.

## 0.6.9 — September 23, 2026

- Browse the homepage by region, from Kanto through Paldea. A region is no longer tied to the generation a Pokémon was introduced in.

## 0.6.8.4 — September 23, 2026

- The dashboard no longer lists progress game by game.

## 0.6.8 — September 23, 2026

- Pokémon sprites appear in the Pokédex, the checklist, and on each Pokémon’s page, including shinies.
- A grayscale sprite fills in with color when you hover its card.

## 0.6.7 — September 23, 2026

- From the homepage you can open a generation and browse it inside your Global Collection, with the same filters on the checklist.

## 0.6.5 — September 22, 2026

- Names, types, evolutions, wild locations, and how-to-obtain notes open faster.

## 0.6.4.1 — September 22, 2026

- The save that tracks every game is now called Global Collection.

## 0.6.4 — September 22, 2026

- Small info buttons on the dashboard and Pokédex explain what a control does. Tap or click to read them.
- “Where to find it” loads faster across the National Pokédex.

## 0.6.3.6 — September 22, 2026

- Retro Pokédex is available on the live site.

## 0.6.3.2 — September 22, 2026

- Importing a Pokémon file keeps the correct gender.

## 0.6.3.1 — September 22, 2026

- Sign in with email. Google and Microsoft sign-in are turned off for now.

## 0.6.3 — September 21, 2026

- Kalos has a region map.
- On the checklist, version exclusives are labeled for the game you have selected (for example, X versus Y).

## 0.6.2 — September 21, 2026

- A checklist for tracking what you have caught and what is still missing.
- Search, filter by status, and switch between the National Pokédex, a regional Pokédex, and the game you are playing.
- Mark a Pokémon caught or shiny from the list.
- Each entry can show a short note on how to get that Pokémon in the selected game.

## 0.6.1 — September 21, 2026

- Region maps use one consistent size for routes, cities, and labels.
- Cities you have not reached yet are easier to see.

## 0.6 — September 21, 2026

- Save the Pokémon you actually own: level, nature, ability, moves, stats, ball, and where you caught it.
- Import one Pokémon file or a batch, then edit or remove those entries.
- Caught and shiny marks are kept per game, separate from the individual Pokémon you import.
- Competitive movesets, plus suggested moves limited to what that Pokémon can learn in the selected game.
- A short overview for each species.
- Reset your password by email.

## 0.5 — September 20, 2026

First release.

- Sign in and keep your own collection.
- Browse the National Pokédex and open any Pokémon for its stats, types, abilities, and moves.
- See type matchups and where a Pokémon appears in the wild.
- Region maps for Kanto, Johto, Hoenn, Sinnoh, and Unova, including a route between locations.
- Track how much of your collection you have caught.
